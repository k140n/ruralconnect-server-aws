\begin{abstract}
Following the successful implementation of the foundational Model-Template-View (MTV) architecture, three-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), guest cart synchronization, and instant artisan publishing in Week 1, the \textbf{RuralConnect} project progressed into its commercialization, intelligence, and platform governance phase during the week of \textbf{November 02, 2026 to November 07, 2026}.

This technical report documents the implementation of major second-phase modules:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item An \textbf{Artisan Business Intelligence \& Analytics Engine} providing real-time time-series sales metrics, multi-stage customer conversion funnels, inventory warnings, and a formal print-ready financial statement/ledger (\texttt{analytics\_report.html}).
    \item A \textbf{Direct WhatsApp Conversational Commerce Pipeline} featuring automated contextual inquiry URL generation and background lead attribution tracking (\texttt{WhatsAppLead}, \texttt{ProductDailyView}).
    \item A \textbf{Multi-Tier Seller Subscription \& Quota Gating Engine} (\texttt{SellerSubscriptionPlan}, \texttt{SellerSubscription}) controlling product creation quotas.
    \item A \textbf{Promotional Boost Lifecycle Engine} with self-healing background synchronization (\texttt{sync\_expired\_boosts}) and an automated CLI daemon (\texttt{expire\_boosts.py}).
    \item \textbf{Administrative Risk Governance} enabling instantaneous seller malpractice suspension cascading to active store listings.
    \item An \textbf{Atomic Transaction-Safe Order Placement \& Tracking Workflow} (\texttt{checkout\_place\_order\_api}) with concurrent stock validation and multi-address support.
    \item A \textbf{Sub-Second Multi-Entity Search Autocomplete Engine} (\texttt{search\_suggest\_api}) paired with a Glassmorphism UI redesign across all customer and management portals.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\section{Detailed Implementation \& Delivered Features}

\subsection{Artisan Business Intelligence \& Analytics Engine}
Rural artisans require transparent visibility into their commercial performance without having to navigate convoluted enterprise software. During this sprint, a comprehensive analytics suite was deployed in \texttt{seller/views.py} and rendered in \texttt{templates/seller/analytics.html}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Core Executive Metrics:} Dynamic calculation of Gross Revenue, Total Units Sold, Total Orders Processed, Average Order Value (AOV), and Return/Cancellation Revenue.
    \item \textbf{Customer Conversion Funnel:} Quantifies buyer drop-off across four explicit operational stages:
    \begin{equation}
        \begin{split}
            \text{Catalog Impressions} &\longrightarrow \text{Customer Engagement (Leads + Wishlists)} \\
            &\longrightarrow \text{Orders Placed} \longrightarrow \text{Delivered Orders}
        \end{split}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Time-Series Revenue \& Traffic Trends:} Date-truncated aggregation (\texttt{TruncDate}) feeding serializable JSON arrays (\texttt{chart\_revenue\_json}, \texttt{chart\_views\_json}) across variable time horizons (7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, Year-to-Date, and All-Time).
    \item \textbf{Regional Provenance Mapping:} Aggregates shipping state information from customer delivery addresses to provide artisans with an empirical geographic breakdown of customer demand across India.
    \item \textbf{Automated Business Insights:} Heuristic rule cards providing instant guidance (e.g., flagging low-stock craft lines, highlighting items with high wishlist demand but zero inventory, and nudging prompt order fulfillment).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Print-Ready Financial \& Performance Statement Engine}
To empower rural craftspeople who interface with local microfinance institutions, artisan cooperatives, or tax authorities, a dedicated printable financial statement view (\texttt{analytics\_report}) was constructed:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Formats all financial figures into a formal, stamp-ready ledger document with dedicated print CSS media queries.
    \item Summarizes net realizable revenue, payment method distribution (UPI vs Cash-on-Delivery), and delivered vs pending consignments.
    \item Provides an itemized transaction ledger recording customer details, dates, unit quantities, and individual order fulfillment statuses.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Conversational Commerce \& WhatsApp Lead Attribution}
A significant barrier for rural artisans is the complexity of digital communication portals. Integrating WhatsApp provides a natural, high-trust communication channel while maintaining platform data attribution:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Contextual URL Generation (\texttt{Product.get\_whatsapp\_inquiry\_url}):} Formats the artisan's registered contact into an E.164 international standard number (e.g., \texttt{+91...}) and builds a prefilled inquiry containing the product title, final discounted price, and direct product URL.
    \item \textbf{Asynchronous Click-Tracking Service (\texttt{track\_whatsapp\_click}):} When a buyer taps the ``Chat with Artisan'' button, an asynchronous AJAX request records a \texttt{WhatsAppLead} record before opening WhatsApp, capturing buyer identity, seller profile, product ID, and timestamp.
    \item \textbf{Daily View Attribution (\texttt{ProductDailyView}):} Tracks daily catalog impressions per product and seller with compound database indexes for sub-millisecond query performance during analytics aggregation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multi-Tier Seller Subscription \& Quota Enforcement}
To establish sustainable platform monetization, a subscription engine was implemented in \texttt{seller/models.py} and integrated into catalog management:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Subscription Model Architecture:} Introduces \texttt{SellerSubscriptionPlan} and \texttt{SellerSubscription}. Plans define duration, price, listing limits (\texttt{max\_products}), and promotional perks.
    \item \textbf{Catalog Creation Gating:} The \texttt{add\_product} and \texttt{edit\_product} views inspect active seller subscriptions via \texttt{seller.has\_active\_subscription()}. Unsubscribed sellers are automatically redirected to plan selection with a preservation query parameter (\texttt{?next=...}).
    \item \textbf{Listing Quota Enforcement:} For tiers with listing constraints (e.g., starter plans), the system verifies current catalog depth against \texttt{plan.max\_products}, prompting artisans to upgrade to the \emph{Heritage Annual Pass} for unlimited inventory slots.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Promotional Boosts \& Automated Expiration Daemon}
To provide verified artisans with heightened storefront visibility without distorting marketplace fairness, a time-bounded promotional boost engine was implemented:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Boost Lifecycle Management:} Artisans purchase promotional boosts (\texttt{BoostPlan}) that elevate their crafts to sponsored positions on the homepage, deal carousels, and search results.
    \item \textbf{Self-Healing Expiration Routine (\texttt{sync\_expired\_boosts}):} Automatically transitions elapsed boosts to \texttt{STATUS\_EXPIRED}, audits affected products, and resets \texttt{Product.promoted = False} unless a concurrent active boost exists.
    \item \textbf{Automated Django Management Command (\texttt{expire\_boosts.py}):} An executable command suitable for crontab scheduling that executes the synchronization routine and outputs summary metrics to standard output.
    \item \textbf{Balanced Feed Sampling:} Homepage curation algorithms randomize and interleave boosted products with organic new arrivals, preventing sponsored listings from monopolizing catalog discovery.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Administrative Governance \& Seller Malpractice Suspension}
To maintain craft authenticity and protect buyers against fraud or non-fulfillment, platform governance was significantly strengthened in \texttt{platform\_admin/views.py}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{One-Click Malpractice Suspension (\texttt{toggle\_seller\_suspension}):} Platform administrators can instantly suspend a seller profile. The action deactivates the user account (\texttt{user.is\_active = False}), instantly terminates their active session via auth decorators, and executes a cascading batch-update marking all their live listings as \texttt{STATUS\_SUSPENDED}.
    \item \textbf{Safe Restoration Protocol:} When an administrative audit clears a seller, the restore action re-enables the user account and safely restores suspended listings back to \texttt{STATUS\_APPROVED}.
    \item \textbf{Granular Catalog Moderation:} The \texttt{manage\_products} console was upgraded with multi-parameter filtering: moderation status, promotional status, category taxonomies, stock-out detection, and price sorting.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Atomic Checkout \& Order Fulfillment Workflow}
During Week 2, the end-to-end purchasing lifecycle was completed from shopping cart to order delivery:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Atomic Order Placement (\texttt{checkout\_place\_order\_api}):} Wrapped in \texttt{transaction.atomic()}, the endpoint validates real-time stock levels, applies authoritative pricing rules, calculates shipping, creates parent \texttt{Order} and individual \texttt{OrderItem} records, decrements product inventory, and empties the customer's cart in a single atomic transaction.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Address Selection:} Integrates with the \texttt{DeliveryAddress} model, allowing buyers to select existing home/office addresses or supply inline delivery details.
    \item \textbf{Order Tracking \& Fulfillment Portals:} Customers monitor order status stages (\emph{Pending}, \emph{Confirmed}, \emph{Shipped}, \emph{Delivered}) on \texttt{my\_orders.html}, while artisans update dispatch timelines directly from their order management dashboard.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multi-Entity Live Search Autocomplete \& UI Modernization}
To accelerate craft discovery and elevate user engagement, the storefront frontend received major performance and aesthetic enhancements:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Multi-Entity Autocomplete API (\texttt{search\_suggest\_api}):} A debounced, lightweight JSON endpoint returning instantaneous tri-facet search suggestions: matching products (with thumbnails and final prices), craft categories (with emojis/slugs), and verified artisan studios (with geographic locations).
    \item \textbf{Glassmorphism Design Overhaul:} Deployed unified CSS design tokens across \texttt{marketplace.css}, \texttt{dashboard.css}, and \texttt{responsive.css}, featuring translucent glass backdrops, mobile-optimized filter drawers, sticky mobile checkout actions, and micro-interaction toast notifications.

\section{Algorithmic Models \& Formulations}

\subsection{Conversion Funnel \& Business Intelligence Metrics}
The seller analytics engine computes the customer conversion rate ($CR$) and Average Order Value ($AOV$) over any selected time window $T$:
\begin{equation}
    CR = \left( \frac{N_{\text{orders}}}{N_{\text{views}}} \right) \times 100\%
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    AOV = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{items}}} \left( P_{k,\text{final}} \times Q_k \right)}{N_{\text{orders}}}
\end{equation}
where $N_{\text{orders}}$ is the total number of distinct orders, $N_{\text{views}}$ represents aggregate page impressions from \texttt{ProductDailyView}, $P_{k,\text{final}}$ is the line item unit price, and $Q_k$ is the unit quantity.

\subsection{Authoritative Boost Expiration Model}
To guarantee that products are only marked promoted when backed by a paid, non-expired boost, the state of the product promotion flag is defined by:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Promoted}(p) = 
    \begin{cases} 
        \text{True}, & \exists \, b \in \mathcal{B}_p : \left( b.\text{status} = \text{active} \land b.t_{\text{start}} \le t \le b.t_{\text{end}} \right) \\
        \text{False}, & \text{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{B}_p$ is the set of all boosts associated with product $p$, and $t$ is the current system date.

\subsection{Subscription Quota Validation Rule}
Product listing authorization is governed by the following conditional rule:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{CanPublish}(s) =\; &\text{HasValidSub}(s) \;\land \\
        &\left( \text{Plan}(s).\text{max\_products} = 0 \lor |\mathcal{P}_s| < \text{Plan}(s).\text{max\_products} \right)
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $s$ is the artisan profile, $\text{HasValidSub}(s) \iff \exists \text{ active subscription with } t \le \text{end\_date}$, and $|\mathcal{P}_s|$ represents the artisan's current catalog size.

\section{Core Implementation Highlights}

\subsection{Automated Boost Synchronization \& Quota Recovery}
\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={Authoritative Boost Expiration Service}]
def sync_expired_boosts():
    """
    Finds active boosts whose end_date has passed, marks them
    as 'expired', and resets Product.promoted=False if the product
    has no other live boost. Cleans orphaned promoted flags.
    """
    today = timezone.now().date()
    past_active_boosts = Boost.objects.filter(
        status=Boost.STATUS_ACTIVE, 
        end_date__lt=today
    )
    affected_product_ids = set(
        past_active_boosts.values_list('product_id', flat=True)
    )

    if past_active_boosts.exists():
        past_active_boosts.update(status=Boost.STATUS_EXPIRED)

    for pid in affected_product_ids:
        has_active = Boost.objects.filter(
            product_id=pid,
            status=Boost.STATUS_ACTIVE,
            start_date__lte=today,
            end_date__gte=today
        ).exists()
        if not has_active:
            Product.objects.filter(pk=pid).update(promoted=False)

    valid_promoted_pids = Boost.objects.filter(
        status=Boost.STATUS_ACTIVE,
        start_date__lte=today,
        end_date__gte=today
    ).values_list('product_id', flat=True)
    Product.objects.filter(promoted=True).exclude(
        id__in=valid_promoted_pids
    ).update(promoted=False)
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Administrative Seller Suspension \& Catalog Cascade}
\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={Seller Suspension Governance Action}]
@admin_required
def toggle_seller_suspension(request, pk):
    seller = get_object_or_404(SellerProfile, pk=pk)
    if request.method == 'POST':
        action = request.POST.get('action')
        if action == 'suspend':
            seller.user.is_active = False
            seller.user.save(update_fields=['is_active'])
            affected = Product.objects.filter(seller=seller)\
                .exclude(status=Product.STATUS_REJECTED)\
                .update(status=Product.STATUS_SUSPENDED)
            messages.warning(
                request,
                f'Seller "{seller.business_name}" suspended. '
                f'Account locked and {affected} listings removed from store.'
            )
        elif action == 'restore':
            seller.user.is_active = True
            seller.user.save(update_fields=['is_active'])
            affected = Product.objects.filter(
                seller=seller, 
                status=Product.STATUS_SUSPENDED
            ).update(status=Product.STATUS_APPROVED)
            messages.success(
                request,
                f'Seller "{seller.business_name}" restored. '
                f'{affected} listings reactivated.'
            )
    return redirect(f"{reverse('platform_admin:dashboard')}?open_sellers=1")
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Atomic Checkout with Concurrent Stock Verification}
\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={Atomic Order Placement Pipeline}]
@require_POST
def checkout_place_order_api(request):
    cart_items = CartItem.objects.filter(user=request.user).select_related('product')
    if not cart_items.exists():
        return JsonResponse({'success': False, 'error': 'Cart is empty'}, status=400)

    with transaction.atomic():
        # Validate stock availability across all line items
        for item in cart_items:
            if item.product.stock < item.quantity:
                return JsonResponse({
                    'success': False,
                    'error': f'Insufficient stock for {item.product.name}'
                }, status=400)

        # Create parent order
        order = Order.objects.create(
            user=request.user,
            shipping_address=selected_address,
            payment_method=payment_method,
            total_amount=calculated_total
        )

        # Generate line items and decrement inventory atomically
        for item in cart_items:
            OrderItem.objects.create(
                order=order,
                product=item.product,
                seller=item.product.seller,
                quantity=item.quantity,
                unit_price=item.product.final_price(),
                line_total=item.product.final_price() * item.quantity
            )
            item.product.stock -= item.quantity
            item.product.save(update_fields=['stock'])

        cart_items.delete()

    return JsonResponse({'success': True, 'order_id': order.id})
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Challenges Solved \& Technical Solutions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Challenge 1: Bridging Digital Literacy with WhatsApp Direct Commerce}\\
    \emph{Problem:} Rural craftspeople frequently abandon complex digital marketplaces because managing web-based customer inquiries is intimidating or technically prohibitive.\\
    \emph{Solution:} Engineered a direct-to-artisan WhatsApp pipeline that generates contextual, pre-filled inquiry links in the customer's mobile app while asynchronously registering lead records (\texttt{WhatsAppLead}) on the backend for sales attribution.
    
    \item \textbf{Challenge 2: Preventing Promotional State Inconsistencies \& Orphaned Boosts}\\
    \emph{Problem:} If an artisan's promotional boost expires at midnight, cached flags or server downtime could cause non-paying products to remain featured indefinitely.\\
    \emph{Solution:} Formulated a deterministic synchronization engine (\texttt{sync\_expired\_boosts}) that evaluates active boosts on demand, resets stale product flags, and operates concurrently via a scheduled CLI command (\texttt{expire\_boosts.py}).

    \item \textbf{Challenge 3: Seamless Subscription Gate Redirection}\\
    \emph{Problem:} Gating product creation behind a paid subscription can frustrate artisans if they fill out a complex listing form only to be blocked upon submission.\\
    \emph{Solution:} Implemented proactive view gates on both \texttt{add\_product} and \texttt{edit\_product} that detect missing or expired subscriptions upon initial entry, redirecting artisans to the subscription tier page with an automatic \texttt{?next=} parameter that resumes listing creation once subscribed.

    \item \textbf{Challenge 4: Race Conditions in Order Inventory Decrement}\\
    \emph{Problem:} Concurrent checkouts on limited-quantity handcrafted items risked selling inventory below zero.\\
    \emph{Solution:} Enclosed order creation, multi-item stock validation, line item generation, and inventory decrements inside a database transaction block (\texttt{transaction.atomic()}) with strict pre-commit verification.
\end{itemize}
